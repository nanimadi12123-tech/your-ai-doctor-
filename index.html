Red Flag Emergency Symptoms

If you or someone else experience any of the following, seek immediate emergency medical evaluation:

  • Crushing chest pain or pressure
  • Sudden numbness or paralysis in face/arm/leg
  • Severe, unmanageable shortness of breath
  • Fainting, sudden confusion or slurred speech
  • High fever with neck stiffness
